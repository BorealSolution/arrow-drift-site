This policy explains what information Arrow Drift (the "app") handles and how.

Accounts

Arrow Drift does not require an account or sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), your IP address, and usage and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement and personalization.

Where required by law (including in the EEA, the UK and certain US states), consent is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in the app under Settings > "Ad privacy options".

To learn how Google handles data from partner sites and apps, see Google's policy.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The developer never receives your payment card details, only a confirmation that a purchase was made.

Data sales

The developer does not sell your data.

Children

Arrow Drift is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.
  • The in-game delete-account option resets the game's local data on your device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the most recent version.
